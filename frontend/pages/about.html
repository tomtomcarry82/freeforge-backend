About FreeForge

FreeForge is a free AI studio in your browser. We believe creative AI tools shouldn't hide behind paywalls, credit systems, or forced signups — so we built the tools we wanted to exist and made them 100% free, with no account required.

What you can do here

  • 🖼️ AI Image Generator — turn text prompts into images in any style, up to 2048px.
  • 🎙️ AI Voice Generator — lifelike text-to-speech in 8 voices across English, Bengali and Hindi; download as MP3.
  • 🎬 Story Video Maker — our signature tool: an AI writes your micro-story, generates a scene image for each beat, narrates it, and assembles a captioned vertical or wide video with cinematic motion — ready for Shorts, Reels, TikTok or YouTube.

How we keep it free

FreeForge runs on free-tier AI services and efficient open-source tooling (FFmpeg for video assembly, disk caching so popular generations are instant). Generous per-visitor fair-use limits stop bots without ever bothering real humans. Our plan is simple: grow a community of creators first, keep the core tools free, and cover costs later with unobtrusive advertising — never by charging creators.

Our promise

We'll never put the core creation tools behind a paywall or a forced login without telling you loudly and clearly. "100% free" is the product — not a trial.
