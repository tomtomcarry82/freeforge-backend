Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

FreeForge ("we") is a free AI creation studio. This policy explains what data we handle when you use FreeForge. In short: we collect almost nothing.

1. No accounts, no tracking profiles

FreeForge requires no signup and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and we do not build profiles about you.

2. What we process to run the tools

  • Your prompts and text. Text you type (image prompts, voiceover scripts, story scenes) is sent to third-party AI services to generate your result: Pollinations (images and story ideas) and Microsoft Edge text-to-speech (voices). We do not sell or share this content for marketing.
  • Generated files. Images, audio and videos are cached temporarily on our server to serve downloads and reduce repeat generation. Cached files are not linked to your identity.
  • Rate-limit counters. To prevent abuse we count requests per IP address (a number, not a person). These counters reset automatically and are never sold or shared.

3. Cookies

FreeForge itself sets no tracking cookies. If we enable advertising in the future, ad partners (such as Google AdSense) may set cookies to serve and measure ads. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the core tools will keep working.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (e.g. page views per tool) to understand which features are popular. This data is statistical only and cannot identify you.

5. Advertising

FreeForge is free to use. In the future we may display ads to cover server costs. Ad networks may use cookies as described in section 3 and operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

FreeForge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Continued use of FreeForge after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the contact page.
