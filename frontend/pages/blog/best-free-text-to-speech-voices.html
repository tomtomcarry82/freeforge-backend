Match the voice to the content

  • Emotional micro-stories: a warm female voice (Ava, Sonia, Tanishaa). Soft sells feelings.
  • Explain / teach: a clear male voice (Andrew, Ryan). Authority keeps attention.
  • Regional audience: always prefer the audience's own language — a Bengali story in Nabanita's voice outperforms the same story in English for a Bangladeshi audience.

Write for the ear, not the eye

TTS reads exactly what you write — so write speakable text:

  • Short sentences. Under 20 words. Long sentences sound breathless.
  • Punctuation is direction. Commas = short pauses, periods = full stops, "!" = energy. Use them deliberately.
  • Spell out numbers and abbreviations. Write "twenty twenty-six", not "2026". Write "doctor", not "Dr."
  • Read it back once. Generate, listen, fix the awkward line, regenerate. Two passes beat one.

Use cases that work today

Faceless YouTube narration, TikTok/Reel voiceovers, podcast intros, audiobooks for your own writing, and voice messages for business pages. Download the MP3 and drop it straight into CapCut or any editor.
