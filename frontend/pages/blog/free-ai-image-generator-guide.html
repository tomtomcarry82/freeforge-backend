Free AI Image Generator: A Beginner's Guide

Image guide · 5 min read

AI image generators turn text into pictures — thumbnails, wallpapers, product mockups, story art. This guide takes you from zero to reliably good results using FreeForge's free generator.

Your first image (60 seconds)

Open the AI Image tab and type something concrete: a red fox in a snowy forest at dawn, photorealistic. Hit generate. That's it — your first AI image. Now let's make the tenth one great.

The 4-part prompt formula

Weak prompts are vague ("a dog"). Strong prompts answer four questions:

  1. Subject — what: a lighthouse
  2. Detail — specifics: on a rocky cliff, waves crashing, seagulls
  3. Style — the look: photorealistic, anime, oil painting, 3D render, watercolor
  4. Mood/light — atmosphere: golden hour, moody fog, neon night

Combined: a lighthouse on a rocky cliff at golden hour, waves crashing below, photorealistic, dramatic sky. Each part you add removes randomness.

Picking the right size

  • Square (768×768) — profile pictures, Instagram posts.
  • Portrait (768×1344) — phone wallpapers, story covers.
  • Landscape (1024×768+) — YouTube thumbnails, banners.

Styles worth trying

Photorealistic for believable scenes; cinematic for drama; anime / ghibli-style for illustration; isometric 3D for cute objects; double exposure for artistic portraits. One style word changes everything — experiment freely, it's free.

Iterate like a pro

Nobody gets the perfect image on try one. Change one thing per attempt (a color, a style word, the lighting) and compare. Save what works — prompt patterns are reusable skills.

A note on the small corner mark

FreeForge uses a free image service that occasionally stamps a tiny attribution mark in the corner. It's the price of free — for thumbnails and social posts, crop slightly or design around it.
