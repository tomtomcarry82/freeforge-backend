FAQ

Is FreeForge really free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup, no credit card. Generous daily fair-use limits keep the service stable for everyone.

Do I need an account or API key?

No. Everything runs in your browser session — type, generate, download.

Can I use the results commercially?

Images are generated by a third-party free service; check their terms for commercial use. Voiceovers and assembled videos you create are yours to use.

How long does a story video take?

A 3–4 scene video usually renders in 2–4 minutes. Longer stories queue the same way — keep the tab open.

Which languages are supported?

Voices cover English (US & UK), Bengali (India & Bangladesh) and Hindi. The AI story writer works best with English topics.

Is Text to Video real AI video generation?

Honestly: no. It generates an AI image from your text, then adds smooth cinematic motion (zoom/pan) to make a 5-second clip. It's a fast, free motion-style video — not native diffusion video like heavyweight studio models.
