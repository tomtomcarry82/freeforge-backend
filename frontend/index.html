EMI Calculator — FAQs

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed monthly payment that repays a loan (principal + interest) over its tenure. Every payment covers that month's interest plus a slice of the principal.

How is EMI calculated?

With the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r the monthly rate, n the number of months. The calculator applies it instantly.

Does the result include processing fees and insurance?

No — the calculator covers principal + interest only. Real loans add processing fees, insurance, and taxes, so treat the output as a planning estimate and confirm final figures with your lender.

Is a longer tenure better?

It lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest paid — sometimes dramatically. Use the calculator to compare total interest across tenures before deciding.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a free estimation tool for planning and comparison. For actual borrowing decisions, speak to your bank or a qualified financial adviser.

Is Text to Video real AI video generation?

Honestly: no. It generates an AI image from your text, then adds smooth cinematic motion (zoom/pan) to make a 5-second clip. It's a fast, free motion-style video — not native diffusion video like heavyweight studio models.
